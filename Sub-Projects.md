# Not Pi Vision Sub-Projects

You can find them slightly in [Templates-divs](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Not-Pi-Vision/tree/main/NPVision-3.1/Fullstack/public/templates-divs)


### [Chem-Flow](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Chem-Flow)  
Visualizes complex chemical reaction mechanisms from text or handwritten topics.

### [Map Video](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Map-Video)  
Creates narrated map journeys using AI and Leaflet.js.

### [Automatic Reader](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Automatic-Reader)  
Enhances static AI-generated study material into **colorful, narrative, or diagrammed formats**.

### [Mindvoice](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Mindvoice)  
Interactive mindmaps with **AI-generated content** and podcast-style narration.

### [Auto-Video-Creator](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Auto-Video-Creator)  
Converts JSON data into narrated videos, graphs, or mindmaps.

### [VR Infusion Layer](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/VR-Infusion-Layer)  
Experimental tool to transform websites into immersive VR experiences *(not successful)*.

---

**Not Pi Vision** tries to integrates these tools to make learning more **visual and interactive**.



## [Video-Like](https://github.com/Sabir-Ali-Mondal/Video-Like)
VIDEO-LIKE: An automatic video creator that uses frontend technologies and text-generating AI to produce 10+ minute detailed documentary-style videos in a single run.
